🔒 Privacy Policy

At Techaphire, accessible from our live network architecture, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Techaphire and how we utilize it under international data protection compliance standards.

1. Log Files & Core Analytical Metrics

Techaphire follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this and a part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

2. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our platform and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the official vendor location.

3. Automated Platform Security

Because our smart utility interface operates through direct client-side script processing arrays, your input operations remain secure within your temporary browser window environment. We maintain rigorous procedural protections to safeguard data integrity across all open-source production structures natively.
