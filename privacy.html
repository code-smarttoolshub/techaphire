🔒 Privacy Policy

At Tech & Mobile Review, the privacy of our visitors is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and how we safeguard your data.

Tech & Mobile Review follows standard procedures of using log files to analyze trends, administer the site, and track user movement. This data includes browser type, ISP, and timestamps, and is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our website. It uses cookies (such as DART cookies) to serve relevant advertisements to visitors based on their visits to this and other websites on the internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.
